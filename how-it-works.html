How it works

From your idea to your door

Four steps, with clear terms at every stage.

1

Tell us what you need

Share the product type, quantity, design or reference photos, and your delivery details. Tell us whether you want a custom range, private label or catalogue styles.

2

We find and check suppliers

We shortlist manufacturers that fit your product and volume, and verify their capacity before recommending them.

3

You approve samples

We arrange samples and prototypes so you can check materials, finish and fit before production starts.

4

We manage production and shipping

We follow production, support on-the-ground inspection, and coordinate shipping to Lagos port.
